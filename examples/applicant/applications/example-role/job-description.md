# Director of Engineering — Wonka Industries

Wonka Industries is hiring a Director of Engineering to lead our platform organization.

## What you will do

- Lead and grow multiple engineering teams.
- Own the reliability and cost of our cloud platform.
- Partner with product on the multi-year technical roadmap.

## Requirements

- 6+ years of software engineering experience, including team leadership. (must)
- Hands-on experience with Python and a systems language such as Go or Rust. (must)
- Experience operating Kubernetes in production. (must)
- Track record of managing cloud infrastructure cost. (nice)
- Experience shipping FDA-cleared medical device software. (must)
