# Identity profile — Jordan Rivera

Who Jordan is beyond the facts. The cover-letter flow draws on this for the letter's
through-line and tone; every fact a letter states still traces to the profile.

## Stories

- **The quiet cutover.** The two-region migration is the story Jordan tells about calm:
  a fifteen-person team, a runbook everyone had rehearsed, and a cutover that ended with
  nobody staying late. Use it when a role values steadiness over heroics.
- **The bill nobody read.** Cutting infrastructure spend by a third started with putting
  the cloud bill in front of every team lead. Use it when a role cares about cost
  transparency or about changing behavior without adding process.

## How to use this

Open a letter on why the role fits the direction above, in Jordan's words, and let one
story carry it. Candor shows in short, plain sentences; stewardship shows in what Jordan
says about the people and systems left behind, never in self-praise.
